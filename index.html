Scope of Services

Four disciplines, handled in-house.

The same team that designs the utility layout is the one standing in front of the MUD board defending it — from concept through the last punch-list item.

01

Site Civil & Land Development

Feasibility studies, land planning, platting, and site design that turns a tract into a buildable subdivision or commercial site.

02

Municipal Utility Infrastructure

Water, sanitary sewer, drainage, grading, and paving design, along with treatment plant, lift station, and re-use system improvements for district facilities.

03

Municipal Utility District Management

Ongoing engineering management for MUD boards — specifications, construction contract documents, construction management, permitting, and plan review.

Parks & Trails

Design and construction oversight for district parks, trails, and green space.

About Us

Founded in 1990, still hands-on today.

Eby Engineers was founded in 1990 by Dennis R. Eby, P.E., a Purdue-trained civil engineer who had previously worked as a project engineer for the City of Houston's Wastewater Engineering Department, McClelland Engineers, and Landev Engineers.

What began as a small civil design practice has grown into the engineering consultant of record for five Harris County Municipal Utility Districts, with more than 2,000 projects spanning water and wastewater treatment facilities, residential and commercial land development, and district parks and trails — while staying intentionally small enough that the engineers who design a project are the ones who see it through construction.

Dennis R. Eby, P.E.
