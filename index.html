Approach

A small firm, on purpose.

Eby Engineers has provided civil design, land planning, and construction management services to the Houston area since 1990. We keep the firm small enough that the engineer who designs your project is the same one who answers the phone during construction.

Every project runs through the agencies that govern it — MUD boards, county offices, and state regulators — and we treat that coordination as part of the engineering, not a step after it.
